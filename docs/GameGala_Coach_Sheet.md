# GameGala 2026 Coach Sheet

## Student Goal
Help the student sound clear, calm, and confident while showing ownership of `flightprint`.

## Winning Formula
- Clear idea
- Smooth demo
- Short strong answers
- Honest explanation
- Calm recovery if anything breaks

## Core Story
`flightprint` helps people see the carbon impact of flights, not just the price.

Main message:
- "I wanted to turn an invisible problem into something people can actually see."

## Best 4-Point Demo Flow
1. `Home`
   Show dashboard, totals, recent flights, and why the app matters.
2. `Add Flight`
   Look up one tested flight example and save it.
3. `Compare`
   Show how the app turns carbon into easier real-life comparisons.
4. `Reports`
   Show monthly and yearly tracking.

## What The Student Should Emphasize
- "I designed this to help people understand the environmental impact of flying."
- "I decided to make the data simple and easy to understand."
- "I wanted users to learn something, not just type information."

## Practice Priorities
- Memorize the first 2 intro sentences.
- Memorize the closing sentence.
- Rehearse the exact click order every time.
- Keep every answer to 1 or 2 sentences.
- Practice speaking slower than feels normal.
- Practice pausing after important points.

## Common Mistakes To Watch For
- Talking too fast
- Reading word-for-word
- Explaining too much code
- Clicking around without a plan
- Saying "we" when the student should say "I"
- Overclaiming what the app does

Important accuracy note:
- The emissions lookup uses real emissions data.
- The booking screen is a planning tool with estimated options before opening a real flight site.

## Confidence Coaching
- Praise specifics, not just effort.
- Use short feedback:
  Keep: one thing that worked well.
  Improve: one thing to fix next round.
- If the student gets stuck, have them restart from:
  "My app helps people understand the carbon impact of flights."

## Mock Judge Routine
- Round 1: normal full run
- Round 2: interrupt with questions
- Round 3: pretend the flight lookup fails

Ask these 5 questions every time:
- How did you build this?
- What was the hardest part?
- Why did you choose this idea?
- What would you improve?
- Did you use AI?

## Recovery Lines
- "I can still explain what this part is supposed to do."
- "I already prepared saved examples, so I can show the result here."
- "The main idea is helping people see carbon clearly."

## Final Day Checklist
- Student already signed in
- Demo account has saved flights
- Exact live example tested
- App open on `Home`
- Needed tabs already open
- Internet checked
- Charger connected
- Notifications off
- Adult supervisor nearby

## Final Reminders For The Student
- Slow down
- Smile at the beginning
- Speak in short sentences
- Click with purpose
- If something breaks, stay calm
- Clarity beats complexity

## Best Closing Line
"I’m proud of this project because it uses coding to help people make smarter environmental choices. Thank you."
